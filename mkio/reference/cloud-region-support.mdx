Each business account on Azure or AWS can create an MK.IO account. Each account can have multiple marketplace subscriptions. Each project you create in your business acount (called **organization**) is attached to one subscription (or method of payment) and one region.

Each project can have different users & admins added to it. (Learn more on [access, users and teams](/mkio/how-to/managing-your-organization/access-users-and-teams).)

| Japan East     | japaneast     |

## Future planned support

If your required region is not available, contact [MK.IO support](https://support.mk.io) or email [sales@mediakind.com](mailto:sales@mediakind.com).
