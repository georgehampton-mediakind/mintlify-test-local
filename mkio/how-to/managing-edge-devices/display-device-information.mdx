<span id="_top" className="mk-legacy-anchor" />

Devices provide detailed information about their status and configuration, including:

* Alarm and contact status: facilitates centralized monitoring.
* Current software version and preloaded software versions that are ready to deploy: enables centralized [software upgrade](/mkio/how-to/managing-edge-devices/update-device-software).
* Network interfaces: displays IP and ASI/SDI interfaces.
