Use AWS Marketplace when you want MK.IO usage billed through an AWS account or covered by an AWS enterprise agreement. Running MK.IO in an AWS location also lets you keep media-processing workloads close to content stored in Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3).

An AWS Marketplace subscription becomes a payment method in MK.IO and provides access to the MK.IO locations available for AWS.

<span id="subscribe-to-mkio" className="mk-legacy-anchor" />

## Subscribe to MK.IO

Open [MK.IO on AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-ngwosmbogkc5c) and complete the subscription workflow provided by AWS.

After the subscription is associated with MK.IO, use it as the payment method for projects that run in an available AWS location. See [Cloud region support](/mkio/reference/cloud-region-support#available-regions-for-aws) for the current location list.

## Prepare the project

1. [Create or open an MK.IO project](/mkio/getting-started/account-setup-and-first-steps).
2. [Connect Amazon S3 storage](/mkio/getting-started/connect-cloud-storage).
3. [Review project access](/mkio/how-to/managing-your-organization/access-users-and-teams).
4. [Review billing and payment methods](/mkio/how-to/managing-your-organization/billing).

You can use subscriptions from more than one cloud marketplace in the same MK.IO organization. Each subscription appears as a separate payment method.

## AI services

When you use MK.IO Artificial Intelligence (AI) services on AWS, an underlying capability may be provided by a third party. All AI use is governed by the MediaKind Data Privacy Agreement.
