## Prepare your account

Complete these setup tasks before a live or VOD workflow requires them:

1. [Set up your account and first project](/mkio/getting-started/account-setup-and-first-steps).
2. [Connect cloud storage](/mkio/getting-started/connect-cloud-storage) for assets, outputs, and recordings.
3. [Learn the MK.IO portal navigation](/mkio/getting-started/platform-navigation-and-key-features).

If your account and storage are already ready, start with one of the workflows above.
