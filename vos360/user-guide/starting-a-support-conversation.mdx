Support conversations are designed to address high-severity cases and to result in the fastest possible recovery. In addition, non-critical issues such as lab issues, and feature/document requests can also be reported through the support conversation.

1. From **Harmonic Hub > Support** click **Start Conversation**.

2. Select the **Product&#x20;**&#x61;nd **System**.

3. Select the **Category or Incident**

   * New Incident (for technical support).

   * Service Request (for non-critical requests).

4. If "New Incident" is requested, selec&#x74;**&#x20;Type&#x20;**&#x74;o notify Harmonic technical support how critical the issue is.

   * Ongoing Incident

   * Restored Incident

5. Specify the issue in the **Description** field.

6. Click the **Start conversation&#x20;**&#x62;utton.\
   ![](/images/vos360/b0cb4bb5e03c11f5abc5ce96.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:&#x20;**&#x49;f the issue has an impact on service, all Harmonic Hub users will receive an incident notification email.
   </Note>

7. Once created, the **Support ticket** screen appears with all the problem details you reported. Click the hypertext in the **Symptom&#x20;**&#x63;olumn to begin a conversation with Harmonic technical support using Slack.

   <Note>
     **Note:&#x20;**&#x48;armonic technical support adopts Slack as a communication channel to deal with your problem in real time.
   </Note>
