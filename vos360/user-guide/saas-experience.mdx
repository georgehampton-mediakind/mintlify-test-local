Harmonic Support teams are committed to providing the best experience for your SaaS systems. In order to ensure the best support and availability times, we put in place the following workflows and internal policies.

## Support workflow

This section describes the overall Harmonic support experience, from proactive notifications from the Harmonic DevOps team to the detailed steps on how to request assistance. We provide technical support through a single case management system anytime, anywhere. Regardless of location or time of day, we work on your request by giving continuous problem resolution and seamless escalation around the clock.

## Proactive communication and engagement

### What is a VOS Incident Notification?

Our DevOps team is actively monitoring your SaaS deployments 24/7/365. Our dedicated monitoring is designed to detect any issue that might impact regular operation on your VOS system. In that scenario, the Harmonic DevOps team will communicate via email that an incident is in progress or a relevant event is identified. Hub users can manually subscribe or unsubscribe from these notifications. "Incident Notifications" are sent to your inbox at any time (24/7/365) for you to be the first to know about any potential active incident and to know the impact it may have on your system, as well as continuous updates about the incident status.

### What happens after the initial VOS incident notification?

The Harmonic DevOps team will update you on the progress, especially while the incident is ongoing. Our DevOps team provides ongoing updates as the investigation progresses until the final restoration. You can rely on us; your system is in the best hands to ensure you can deliver your customers the best service. This scenario will activate an incident lead to ensure a fast recovery and rich communication during troubleshooting.

### Can I communicate with the Harmonic team working on the incident?

Sure, of course you can. We are here to help you. If you would like to start a live conversation with Harmonic tech teams, you can do so at any time. That immediately connects you with our professional teams. These conversations are opened during service restoration and initial communication. Any follow up contact required during root cause investigation uses the Hub and email until the incident is fully understood.

### What is the Harmonic team monitoring?

As part of SaaS offerings, all VOS360 instances are proactively monitored. This ensures up-time commitment that helps us deliver the best service.

## Engaging Harmonic support

From the Harmonic Hub portal, you have the unique ability to communicate with us at any given time. You can use this opportunity to report any ongoing issues or request assistance on any problem you might have.

See [Contacting Harmonic technical support](/vos360/user-guide/contacting-technical-support) for more information.

## Case Lifecycle

Upon service request, live chat communication will be established. In case of an ongoing incident, communication will remain open during the service restoration stage. Once service is **recovered**, live chat will be archived and communication will be moved to Harmonic HUB or email alternatively. Case status can be <u>checked at any time</u> from the HUB portal. One case will be created on our CRM to keep track of and investigate any incidents or questions you may want to bring up.

![](/images/vos360/d43eb87f91c7e45403c7e1a0.png)

From the case creation until the case closure, our teams will be working on the problem restoration, first, and the investigation and analysis later.

## What is Slack?![](/images/vos360/d3125197db6f1e177fa8a39a.png)

Slack is a messaging app for businesses that enable you to directly connect to all our Support teams 24/7.

You may open a slack channel by directly opening a Support ticket **from the Hub**, or by following the instructions on the Incident Notification email **after an incident** has been identified and is currently being investigated by our team.

After the Slack conversation channel is closed (**when the issue is restored**), the case will still be browsable and checked in the Hub (*Support* section) for more detailed resolution notes and root cause analysis.

## Evaluating and resolving the problem

### Incident team activation

In the event of an impacting incident at any hardware/software bit in the VOS360 ecosystem, our Incident Team will be activated to ensure fast and reliable resolution.

The **incident team** is activated when an incident is ongoing and needs restoration. This team consists of a group of up to four individuals with specific roles and responsibilities. When the team is initially activated, the Incident Team will look like this:

* Incident Lead

* DevOps T1 Engineer

* SRE Engineer

If the goal is not achieved in the targeted time, additional members will join the Incident Team.

* R\&D Dev Engineer (if needed)

The Incident Lead will drive the different team's collaboration as well as be the leader providing current status updates to you through Slack channel.

![](/images/vos360/b0c1b3eacb5ef32b72debab9.png)

### Escalation and points of contact

The Harmonic DevOps team provides 24/7/365 support coverage through our online Harmonic Hub portal. Should you feel your case has not been given the correct priority or escalation, contact the Technical Customer Success Manager or DevOps Support Management.

### Post-mortem investigation

Some cases might face or report a complex incident that needs some in-depth follow up. These cases might lead to internal engagement with our R\&D teams to perform code modifications. If so, any communications and updates happen quickly in the Hub. This allows you to stay updated on the latest status of a relevant investigation.

### Tracking incidents and requests

Active and past requests are available in the Hub. This allows you to review and track notes and relevant data for any case that has been handled by our support teams. You can also **ask questions** to our teams by leaving a comment in any Hub ticket, even if it doesn't have an active Slack conversation. Our team will review and answer your questions via HUB ticket comments.
