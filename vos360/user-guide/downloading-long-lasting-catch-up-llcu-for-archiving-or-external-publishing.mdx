## Exporting an MP4 file to publish to social networks

1. Navigate to **Asset Acquisition library** and open the LLCU asset you want to publish.

2. Click on the **Video assets** tab in the popup window, hover on **Publish** and click **Request download**.

   ![image-20260107-173108.png](/images/vos360/e6c17f3319936754ac8cf8e6.png)

3. After a short time an MP4 file is ready for download

   ![image-20260107-173124.png](/images/vos360/9038378fd4c15cff4e5ee8e0.png)
