robots: noindex, nofollow
---

You can add a custom logo to your channel. To do so, do the following:

1. From the Channels app, click **+ Channels**.

2. Click **Configure** next to the Logo toggle.

3. Click to turn the toggle **On**.

4. Select the logo you want to use by clicking the pencil icon.

5. Click on an image to use for your channel.

6. Click **Apply**.

7. In the **Placement** area, select the desired position of the logo.

8. In the **Offset** area, indicate the horizontal and vertical offset (relative the Placement) in pixels.

9. Click **Save**.

You can also manage images for your channel. To do so, do the following:

1. From the Channels app, click **+ Channels**.

2. Click **Configure** next to the Logo toggle.

3. Click to turn the toggle **On**.

4. Select the **Manage images** tab.

5. Click **+ image** to navigate to a saved image.

6. Click![](/images/vos360/7e78e98aaab3db2c5b0abf05.png) to view the image full size.

7. Click![](/images/vos360/b5dd87a1d68dee127c4b1b8d.png)to copy the URL of the image.

8. Click **Delete** to remove an image.

9. Click **Save Changes**.
