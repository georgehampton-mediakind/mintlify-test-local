IPTV services are delivered over IP as transport streams. In an IPTV setup, operators receive compressed streams from content providers via satellite, terrestrial broadcast and IP circuits. These incoming video services will be groomed and transcoded by VOS for delivery to set-top boxes in HD and SD and connected devices.

The video and audio elements inside the transport streams can contain the following codecs:

* Video: AVC, HEVC

* Audio: MPEG-1 L2, AAC, Dolby
