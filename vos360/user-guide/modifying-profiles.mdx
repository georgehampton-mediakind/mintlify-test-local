title: Modifying profiles
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

1. From the Lab Wizard app, double-click the profile to open the **Profiles editor**.

   ![lab\_wizard\_profile\_editor.png](/images/vos360/d05cfeaa2e49d88fdd060b6b.png)

2. Click the item you want to modify in the **Configured** pane.

3. Make any modifications in the **Relevant Specs** pane.

4. If the **Customize Specs** dialog box opens, do one of the following:

   * To continue editing, click **Yes, Override to Manual Mode**. **Manual mode** enables you to override the profile's default settings.

   * To cancel editing and revert to the default settings, click **No, Keep Parametric Mode**. **Parametric mode** maintains the default settings configured by Harmonic.

5. Optionally, enter a brief comment to associate with the profile changes.

6. Click **Save&#x20;**&#x74;o overwrite the previous version of the profile.\
   You can also click **Save as** to save the changes as a different profile.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x41;fter a profile has been updated, you cannot revert your changes.
</Note>

**Result**: On the **Profile panel**, the **Customer Version Number** increases by 1.
