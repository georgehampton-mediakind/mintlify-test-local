## Harmonic 24/7/365 DevOps support

Harmonic provides industry-leading 24/7/365, geographically dispersed DevOps support. Leveraging virtually co-located teams, the advanced software foundation and telemetry capabilities of our solutions, a robust global communication network, and advanced remote monitoring and management tools, our global technical teams provide you with the best support experience.

![](/images/vos360/5e74d94af58e70f19c4401c7.png)

The Harmonic support teams are based on a tiered model, which helps to efficiently manage and address customer inquiries and requests.

### DevOps T1 (T1)

Our DevOps T1 team is the first support line you connect with when raising a support ticket. DevOps T1 is responsible for initial communication, system monitoring (for VOS360 SaaS only), incident assessment, troubleshooting, fast restoration, and internal engagement. The strong technical skills of the team will make your Harmonic experience successful.

### Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)

The Site Reliability Engineering team is the second support level and assists in highly complex incidents and investigations. This team also handles system operations in SaaS environments, such as system tuning, deployments, or upgrades. Their product-specific expertise is essential to ensure system uptimes and help address complex technical problems.

### Research & Development (R\&D)

And finally, on the highest Tier level, the Harmonic Developer team is the engineering group that actively develops our high-technology solutions and maintains that code for changes and bug fixes.

Following DevOps best practices, all the Harmonic teams collaborate to provide the highest quality products and services.

## Technical Customer Success Managers

As part of the team with defined responsibility for your success, your Technical Customer Success Manager (TCSM) is your primary point of contact post-sale. With a consistent presence, they proactively engage with your teams throughout your entire platform lifecycle.

The TCSM is the glue between Harmonic teams. They act as customer advocates, support you for escalations, work with you to facilitate any service requests you have, partner with you to discuss and mutually agree on your desired outcomes, and plan the journey to ensure these outcomes are successful.

## Cloud Solutions Delivery (CSD)

Cloud Solution Delivery is the technical customer support during the VOS projects cycle and the post-project services.

Our mission is to deploy and configure operational-ready VOS platforms and deliver VOS services, maintaining alignment with the strategic goals and mission of the SaaS delivery business at Harmonic.
