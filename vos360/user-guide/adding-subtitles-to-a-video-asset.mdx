---
title: Adding subtitles to a video asset
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Asset Acquisition supports uploading sidecar subtitles including SRT, STL, SCC, NCI, PAC, WebVTT, Cheetah files, and DVB Subtitles, up to 50 MB.

<Note>
  **Info:&#x20;**&#x54;he sidecar PAC subtitle (.pac file type) is supported for VOD transcoding and OTT packaging to WebVTT and TTML text (Eng, Arb, Fra) output.
</Note>

1. From the **Library** tab, select the asset you want to edit.

2. Click the **Subtitles** tab, and browse to and select the subtitle file you want to add, or drag and drop the subtitle file.

3. Set the subtitle **Language**.

4. Set the subtitle **Type**. You can choose from the following:

   * Normal

   * Hard of hearing

     <Note>
       **Info:&#x20;**&#x53;idecar subtitles files can be marked as audio descriptions for hard-of-hearing accessibility. The corresponding tagging of the subtitles track is done in OTT playlists for VOD.
     </Note>

5. Optionally, add more subtitle files.

   <Note>
     **Info:&#x20;**&#x59;ou may add up to 4 subtitle files per video asset.
   </Note>

6. Click **Save**.

   <Note>
     **Important:&#x20;**&#x41;fter you add subtitles to a video, you must re-transcode the asset.
   </Note>

7. From the **Library** tab, select the same video asset.

8. From the **Video Assets** tab, find the desired transcoding profile and then click **Generate**.

9. Click **Generate now** to confirm.\
   **Result:** The asset is sent to the **Ready** queue for processing, and then to the **Jobs** queue. The new asset will be available in the **Library** when the job is complete.

   <Note>
     **Note:&#x20;**&#x44;ue to CDN caching, the video available for preview might not update right away.
   </Note>
