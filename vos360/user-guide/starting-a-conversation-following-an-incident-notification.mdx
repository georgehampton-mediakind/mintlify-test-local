---
title: Starting a conversation following an incident notification
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Incident notifications are sent for issues impacting service. Upon receipt of a notification, you may engage with Harmonic technical support to ask questions or receive updates related to the incident.

1. From the incident notification email, click the hyperlink in the Next Steps section to start a conversation with Harmonic technical support.

2. Log in to the Harmonic Hub, and then click **Incidents**.\
   **Result:** The **Start support conversation** appears and the **Category or Incident** field is pre-populated with the incident ID.

3. In the **Description** field, enter your question or comment related to the incident, and then click **Start conversation**.

4. From the **Support conversation started** page, click the hypertext in the **Description** column to begin a conversation with Harmonic technical support using Slack.\
   ![](/images/vos360/7f5e6c73cca9494f15868ca6.png)

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x48;armonic technical support adopts Slack as a communication channel to deal with your problem in real-time.
</Note>
