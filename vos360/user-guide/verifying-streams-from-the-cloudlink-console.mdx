Verify and monitor multiple bitrate (MBR) streams from the CloudLink **Status** panel.

1. On the remote server, click **Windows > Start > VMware vSphere Client**.\
   The **VMware vSphere Client** dialog opens.

2. Enter the management IP address, username, and password for the virtual machine, and then click **Login**.

3. In the **Inventory** pane, expand the IP address of the virtual machine.

4. Select the CloudLink virtual machine IP address, and then click **Launch Virtual Machine Console**.\
   **Result**: The vSphere virtual machine console opens.

5. Click anywhere on the screen and then press **Tab** twice to move the cursor to the **Login** field.

6. Enter your Harmonic ID and then press **Tab** once to move the cursor to the **Password** field.

7. Enter your Harmonic ID password and then press **Tab** once.

8. Press **Enter**.

9. In the **Status** panel, verify that your CloudLink is registered and that four active MBR video streams are downlinked.
