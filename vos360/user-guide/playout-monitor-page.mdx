### Full view

![vos\_playout\_monitor\_full\_view\_full.png](/images/vos360/a844ad01489fc1e259a1db7e.png)

Use the **View&#x20;**&#x74;oggle to switch to the Full view. A dialogue window appears confirming you want to enter the Full view mode. Acknowledge it to proceed. Once acknowledged, a progress bar appears while the events in the list are loading. You cannot make any modifications while the content is loading.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x54;he timeline is not available in Full view.
</Note>
