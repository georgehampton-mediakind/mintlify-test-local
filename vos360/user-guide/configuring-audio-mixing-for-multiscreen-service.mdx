title: Configuring audio mixing for multiscreen service
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x54;o enable the audio mixing feature, you must have a Lab Wizard profile that has an audio profile with audio mixing settings. To do so, you must load the Lab Wizard profile for audio mixing through the VOS API.
</Note>

1. From th&#x65;**&#x20;Configure Channels** app, click on your source.

2. In the **Inputs** section, click on the **Groom** menu, and select **Data Services**.

3. Tag your streams with an appropriate title to enable mixing of the streams.

4. Click **OK**.
