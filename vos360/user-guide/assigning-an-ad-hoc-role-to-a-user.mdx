---
title: Assigning an ad hoc role to a user
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

An ad hoc role is a unique role assigned to a particular user who requires custom access to VOS apps.

1. From the Users & Roles app, click **Users**.

2. From the **User Accounts** list, click the name of the user receiving the ad hoc role.

3. From the **User Account** pane, select **Role > Ad Hoc Role**.

4. Select the apps and app options that will be available to the user and click **Save**.
