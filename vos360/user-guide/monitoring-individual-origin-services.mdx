You can monitor delivery statistics for the global system or individual services through the dashboard from the Origin app.

## **Status tab**

* Enables you to monitor baseline delivery statistics.

* For each metric, use the ![](/images/vos360/d13070f1d8b2cb6abba31a25.png) button to monitor statistics on full screen or with additional details.

* Change the **Time Range** to monitor the time period of interest.

* Circular buffer health monitoring is available at the bottom for individual time-shift (Start-over, Catch-up, Long-Lasting Catch-up) services.

  ![](/images/vos360/f96ee31be111841a1c0d96aa.png)
