Different encryption modes and DRM systems are supported depending on the OTT output format.

Key rotation is supported for HLS, DASH, CMAF and HSS.

(\*) Key rotation is not supported due to format-specific limitation.

(\*\*) Live only

Note: Several protection schemes are defined in MPEG-CENC standard:

* MPEG-CENC 'cbc1' protection scheme = AES-128 CBC mode, with full encryption

* MPEG-CENC 'cbcs' protection scheme = AES-128 CBC mode, with partial encryption

* MPEG-CENC 'cenc' protection scheme = AES-128 CTR mode, with full encryption

* MPEG-CENC 'cens' protection scheme = AES-128 CTR mode, with partial encryption
