title: Connecting sources to the multiplexer
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

You can connect the source to the multiplexer that you have created in the Configure Broadcast app.

1. From the **Source&#x20;**&#x70;anel on the left, drag and drop the source to the desired **Multiplexer&#x20;**&#x6F;n the right.

   ![](/images/vos360/8d1fa27bc2c67e8c1efd5f55.png)

2. Select the way you want to process from the **Connect source to mux** window. \
   ![](/images/vos360/b6952b816c5fa331cbaf979b.png)

3. Click **Connect** to confirm.

4. Once the connection between the source and the mux is successfully created, click the **Edit Multiplexer&#x20;**&#x69;con to verify.\
   ![](/images/vos360/7a3243f50f9c6ba05040f4ad.png)
