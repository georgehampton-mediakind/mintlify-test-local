Log in to VOS360 through the Harmonic Hub. Harmonic requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) using Harmonic Okta for all Hub and VOS360 users.

See the *Harmonic Hub User Guide* on the [Harmonic Documentation Portal](https://techlibrary.harmonicinc.com/) for more information.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x43;ontact your administrator if you encounter issues with the Okta MFA or need to reset your password.
</Note>

## Initial log in

The first time you log on to the Harmonic Hub, a **Set up multifactor authentication** window appears.

1. Use the following link to access Hub: [https://www.harmonicinc.com/portal-logins](https://www.harmonicinc.com/portal-logins).

2. Click **Setup.**

3. Follow the instructions [here](https://support.okta.com/help/s/end-user-adoption-toolkit/setting-up-mfa-for-end-users?language=en_US) to download and setup th&#x65;**&#x20;Okta Verify** app.

## Subsequent log ins

Each time you log in to Harmonic Hub, you must sign in using your Okta credentials. Do the following:

1. Open the **Harmonic Hub** or **VOS system** on your computer.\
   The **Okta Verify** window appears.

2. Click **Log In**.

3. Tap the notification that appears on your mobile device.

4. Tap **Yes, It's Me&#x20;**&#x74;o confirm and log in.

5. From the Managed Accounts tab, navigate to your VOS360 instance and click the eye icon to open it.

6. (Optional) Click on the name of your VOS360 instance to view any preferences or configurations.
