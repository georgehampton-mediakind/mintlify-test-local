robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Register and name a standalone CloudLink with a VOS360.

1. From the **Configure** **Channels&#x20;**&#x61;pp, click the **Show CloudLink** icon.

2. For the CloudLink you wish to register, click **Register / Pair**.

3. From the **Register CloudLink** dialog box, select **Single**, type a new name for the CloudLink, and then click **Save**.
