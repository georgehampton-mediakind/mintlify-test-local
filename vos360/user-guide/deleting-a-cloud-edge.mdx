---
title: Deleting a Cloud Edge
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

1. From the Cloud Sources tab, click ![](/images/vos360/c5178820e3ab4a6ff77d5de6.png) on the tile of the channel you want to delete.\
   The Edit window appears.

2. Click **Delete**.\
   A confirmation window appears.

3. Click **Confirm**.
