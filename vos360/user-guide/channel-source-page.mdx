The **Channel source** page is the landing page for the Playout Monitor app. It displays a timeline for each playout service.

To view scheduled events, load schedules, and edit the on-air schedule, click the **Playout Source**.

Two view modes are available. Click the **Switch viewing mode** button at the top right to choose your preferred playout channel view.

* Timeline mode

* Status monitoring mode

## Timeline mode

The Timeline mode lets you view your playout channels in a scrolling timeline.

![playout\_monitor\_channels\_update.png](/images/vos360/164ef268926666510b6bf99b.png)

Choose from these options:

* Use the filter field to search playout channels.

* Click the **Playout source** title to open the channel on the **Playout monitor** page.

## Status monitoring mode

Status monitoring mode lets you view your playout channels in a grid layout.

![status\_monitoring\_view\_3\_3\_7.png](/images/vos360/6892b4c946d4be97c20c1f95.png)

Choose from the following grid layout options:

* 2x2

* 3x3 (default)

* 4x3

* 5x4

Each channel box includes:

* Channel name

* Channel preview

* On Air information and Take Next\
  **Note:** The Edit toggle must be **On** to enable the Take Next action.

* Any errors associated with the channel

* Audio and audio bar graph

You can also do the following with each channel box:

1. Click the channel name to open it on the **Playout monitor** page.

2. Double-click the channel preview to open a larger version.
