---
title: Registering a CloudLink from the Terminal UI (OVA installation)
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Use the Terminal UI to select the VOS360 you wish to associate with the CloudLink.

1. From the Terminal UI, click anywhere on the console screen and press the **Tab** key until the orange highlight moves to the **Login** field.

2. In the **Login** field, type your Harmonic ID and then press **Tab**.

3. In the **Password** field, type your Harmonic ID password and then press **Tab**.

4. Press **Enter**.

5. Press the **Tab** key until you have selected the VOS360 that the CloudLink is to be associated with, then press **Enter**.\
   **Result**: After login, the Terminal UI shows the default CloudLink name and the registration status in the upper right corner of the console screen.

\
&#x20;**What to do next**\
&#x20;Log in to VOS and complete the registration process in the Channels app.
