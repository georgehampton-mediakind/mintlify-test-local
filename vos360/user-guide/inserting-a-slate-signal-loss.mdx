---
title: Inserting a slate (signal loss)
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

When signal loss occurs, a slate can replace the video.

From **Configure Broadcast** app, at multiplexer level:

1. Click the Edit button of the program.

2. From the source tab, enable **Signal Loss Slate** (**ON**)

3. To **Display a black screen** in case of signal loss, set it to **ON**.

   ![](/images/vos360/d160836089710494bc3df84e.png)

4. To insert a customized slate click edit button at signal loss image level.

5. Click ***+*****Image** to select from the C\&C PC the slate to insert.\
   \
   &#x20;![](/images/vos360/cbbc0a4a4010f5d939cb2cf3.png)

6. Set the **Path** to download the picture.

7. Define a **Label** to identify the picture easily.

8. Click **Add**.

9. Select the slate that will replace the video in case of signal loss.\
   \
   ![](/images/vos360/ca383c6424b4d594f92e9939.png)

10. Click **Apply**.

11. Click **Save**.

12. After few seconds the slate appears on the thumbnail of the program.

![](/images/vos360/4d86714f47bd700d5366c358.png)
