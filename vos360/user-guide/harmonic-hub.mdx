Harmonic Hub is the online administration console for all your connected Harmonic products and services.

* Manage a wide range of aspects including user IDs, transcoding profiles, software downloads and daily backups.

* Start a new conversion to receive support and benefit from our expertise.

* Enhance operational efficiency through our online dashboards and reporting systems.

* Connect your other Harmonic products, to reduce troubleshooting time and increase your service uptime.

Hub connected systems\
![](/images/vos360/06e963b4eba94ee6f0cea2aa.png)
------------------------------------------------

The Harmonic Hub can be reached at [https://hub.harmonicinc.com](https://hub.harmonicinc.com/).

* As an online SaaS it is operated and maintained by Harmonic.

* Connectivity to Hub is essential for performing some VOS360 operations such as creating and managing Harmonic IDs, requesting support etc.

* Multi-factor authentication (MFA) through Okta is required for Hub access.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x41; public internet connection is required for communication between the client PC and the Harmonic Hub
</Note>
