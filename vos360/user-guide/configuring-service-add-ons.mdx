&#x20;The availability of service add-ons depends on the type of service you have configured. Before enabling service add-ons, you must create a service from an existing source, transcoding profile, and destination.

Configure add-ons as required by the service:

1. Navigate to **Configure Channels&#x20;**&#x61;pp > **Services**.

2. Enable the **Add-ons&#x20;**&#x63;heckbox.

   ![](/images/vos360/069f3cbf1bca10dfb8c4ef33.png)

3. Turn **ON** your desired add-ons with specific configurations.

## **Slate A/B Watermark**

To generate two sets of output with the same content except one with watermark slate A inserted and another one with watermark slate B inserted, enable the **Slate A/B Watermark&#x20;**&#x61;dd-on.

Choose from the following options:

* **ViaccessOrca:** Link with the Viaccess-Orca backend (Viaccess Campaign Manager) to retrieve the slate A and B automatically.

* **Generic:** Select the slate A and slate B images manually through the VOS UI. Slate images must be provisioned on VOS360. See [Configuring a signal loss image](/vos360/user-guide/configuring-the-signal-loss-input) for more information on how to provision the slates.

<Note>
  **Info:&#x20;**&#x59;ou need to turn on the **OTT Watermarking&#x20;**&#x61;nd set the "**Alt Watermarking Output Prefix**" (e.g. "b.") in the [Destination Profile](/vos360/user-guide/streaming-and-broadcast-destination-profile-parameters) for HLS, DASH, and CMAF packaging outputs. In addition, you need to configure **Output Mode&#x20;**&#x61;s "**Live Only**" in the [multiscreen/origin service destination](/vos360/user-guide/configuring-a-multiscreen-origin-service-destination).
</Note>

## **Kantar Snap Watermarking**

To insert the Kantar watermarking into your service, enable the **Kantar Snap Watermarking** add-on. (This add-on is visible only if the Kantar Product and Audience license files have been loaded to the VOS system on the [Settings](/vos360/user-guide/settings) page.)

Configure the Kantar Product and Audience licenses for the audio channels. (Refer to [Configuring Kantar audio watermarking](/vos360/user-guide/configuring-kantar-audio-watermarking) for details.)

## Synamedia Watermark

If the service supports Synamedia Watermark in the encoded stream, enable the **Synamedia Watermark** add-on and configure an valid **Channel ID**.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x54;o make this add-on visible you must first set the parameter '**isEnable**' of '**globalSynamediaWatermarkSetting**' through REST-API. This add-on is only shown on the service with **Multiscreen&#x20;**&#x70;rofile.
</Note>

## Operator Action

To define the Start and End times for transcoded services, enable the **Operator Action&#x20;**&#x61;dd-on.

* Turn on the **Mute Operation&#x20;**&#x74;oggle under the **Operator Action&#x20;**&#x61;dd-on to schedule or manually start and stop streaming on the output through the **Monitor Channels&#x20;**&#x61;pp. (Refer to [Scheduling streaming start and end times in the Monitor Channels app](/vos360/user-guide/scheduling-streaming-start-and-end-times-in-the-monitor-channels-app) for details.)
