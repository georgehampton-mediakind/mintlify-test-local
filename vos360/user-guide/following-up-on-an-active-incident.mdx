After the incident is created (either from Harmonic side or the customer's) the active communication is followed via the **Slack channel**. You are able to provide any information and files in the slack channel (video captures, logs, images, etc.) as well as request further information or updates on the restoration status. At all times, our DevOps team will be pending your questions and requests. Periodic updates will be provided from our side.

## 24/7 DevOps T1 response

Your first point of contact with Harmonic Support will be the DevOps T1 team. This team is working non-stop to be ready to take action on any possible incident that may impact your system.

Our team is available at any time via Slack conversation tickets: ![](/images/vos360/d3125197db6f1e177fa8a39a.png)

## Incident Team escalation

In case necessary, our Incident team will be activated (*see&#x20;*[*SaaS Experience - Incident team activation*](https://confluence360.video.harmonicinc.com/wiki/spaces/CS/pages/119512834/SaaS+Experience#SaaSExperience-Incidentteamactivation)*&#x20;for further details*).

In this situation, the Incident Lead will take a central role in providing updates from our Support teams and ensuring the problem is resolved in a timely manner.
