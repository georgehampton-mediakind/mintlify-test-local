<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x49;n-browser live video monitoring and previewing is not available in Apple devices running iOS. Instead of displaying the video, the player appears but displays a black screen. To work around the issue, use a browser on a different device.
</Note>

1. From the **Channels** app, select the **Watch** tab.

2. Click on the created channel.

3. Click the **Timeshift** button at the top right corner.

   ![](/images/vos360/e9013d66c918ed168d55750f.png)

4. Configure the Start Time and Duration.

5. Click **Preview**.
