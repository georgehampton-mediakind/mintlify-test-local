robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Use the Configure Channels app to view or modify the CloudLink settings.

1. From the Configure Channels app, click the **Sources** page.

2. Click the **Show CloudLink** icon to display your CloudLink instances.

3. Click **Edit** on the CloudLink panel to view or modify the CloudLink.
