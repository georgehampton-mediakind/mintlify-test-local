---
title: Registering paired CloudLink instances
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Pair up CloudLink instances and register the pair with a VOS360 for redundancy purposes. If the primary CloudLink is unavailable, transport streams will fail over to the standby.

1. From the **Configure Channels** app, click the **Show CloudLink** icon.

2. For the CloudLink you wish to register, click **Register / Pair**.

3. From the **Register CloudLink** dialog box, click **Pair**, select the CloudLink you wish to add to the pair, and then click **Save**.

   <Note>
     **Note:&#x20;**&#x59;ou cannot unpair CloudLink instances after the association is saved. You must delete the paired CloudLink instances and re-register them as single instances to unpair them.
   </Note>
