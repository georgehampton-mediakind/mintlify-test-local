title: Registering a CloudLink
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

You must register CloudLink instances with a VOS360 before you can transmit video.

Registering a CloudLink is a two-part process. You must first register it in the TUI and then complete the process in the Channels app.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x48;armonic recommends that you use the Client Credentials flow to register CloudLink instances. Follow instructions for [Registering an OAuth client application](/vos360/user-guide/registering-an-oauth-client-application), and then complete Cloudlink registration.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x57;hen registering a CloudLink with a VOS360, you may register it as a single, standalone instance or as a pair of instances. If you wish to convert a single, registered CloudLink to a paired instance, you must first forget the CloudLink and then re-associate it with VOS360.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x45;ach CloudLink can be registered with only one VOS360.
</Note>
