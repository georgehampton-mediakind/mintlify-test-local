## Hub experience

Through our online Harmonic Hub connected experience, you have the unique ability to start a support conversation within the Hub, which provides you direct access to our 24/7/365 DevOps team. This allows for a unique, real-time mode of communication for service-impacting incidents, ensuring that updates during incidents are easily communicated through a quick engagement process.

## Hub overview

The Harmonic Hub is a business intelligence (BI) solution for VOS through our online management platform, which provides a connected experience, available 24/7. The Hub allows you to control your expenses with complete access to monitor your usage and costs through your dashboard, pricing, and invoices.

With the Harmonic Hub, you can access everything you need to produce, deliver, and manage exceptional experiences for your subscribers.

Using the Hub, you can start, track, and monitor support any cases you have raised to support. The Hub also provides an overview of incidents that may have impacted or are impacting the platform.

### Quick overview of Hub sections

1. **Welcome**: Landing area with a quick overview of your deployed systems, active incidents, support conversations, and available software. Any important notification about your system(s) will be displayed on top of this page.

2. **Software**: Prepare your own CloudNative or CloudLink deployment. The software will be available for you to download from your portal. Contact your local Harmonic representative for more details.

3. **System**: Review your deployed infrastructure details, status, and billing data. Grant access to your system users based on the desired role profile.

4. **Harmonic ID**: Identity Access and Management of your users.

5. **Support**: Report, review, and track any incidents or cases that might impact your system. Start a new conversation with our Support teams providing a summary of your problem.

6. **Resources**: Access to VOS360 training and documentation portal through the Harmonic learning platform.
