**What is VOS360 Media SaaS Live Streaming Platform and VOS360 Media SaaS Channel Origination and Distribution Platform?**

VOS®360 Media SaaS (software as a service) is the most agile media processing SaaS platform for OTT, channel origination & distribution 2.0.

VOS360 is a SaaS solution that is hosted in the public cloud and maintained and monitored by Harmonic. It lets you run leaner, more agile and scalable operation. VOS360 SaaS allows you to create and deliver revenue-generating, broadcast-quality OTT streaming services, including linear channel origination, live, SVOD, and time-shift TV directly to your viewers on any screen in a matter of hours instead of months. Simply define your sources, upload your assets, and set regional or global distribution policies to start engaging with your audience.

**VOS360 Capabilities**

* **Linear Channel origination**\
  VOS360 enables channel origination for linear and pop-up channels for special events with live programming and replay of aired clips.

* **Live streaming**\
  Securely contribute live 24/7 services and events and deliver directly to consumers with low latency **OTT streaming. You can securely stream live channels or events to any screen. VOS360 prepares and delivers MPEG-DASH, Apple® HLS, and Microsoft® Smooth Streaming directly to your customer’s devices.**

* **SVOD**\
  Quickly and securely ingest your existing SVOD library and VOS360 will prepare and deliver your catalog directly to your streaming subscribers.

* **Time-shift TV**\
  Simply define your recording window for each channel, whether a couple of hours or several months. Allow start-over capability for TV shows that are currently playing, or go back in the program guide to watch shows that aired previously, giving consumers complete control over their content consumption desires.

* **Cloud DVR**\
  Allow your customers to record individual TV shows or series, perform impulsive recording and consume the content on any device, anytime, anywhere.

* **Global distribution**\
  VOS360 delivers content directly to your consumers via a global CDN. The service provides management of local and global distribution policies, whitelisting and blacklisting.

* **Primary distribution**\
  Prepare and distribute OTT-ready local broadcast channels to MVPDs using VOS360 and meet the growing demand for localized video content on any connected device.

* **Teleport services**\
  Harmonic can optionally provide satellite and IP reception and contribution services anywhere in the world via an array of international partner agreements.
