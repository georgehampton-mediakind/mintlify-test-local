---
title: Testing a CloudLink uplink from the Configure Channels app
robots: noindex, nofollow
---

Start the stream and then verify video output in the Configure Channels app.

1. From the CloudLink TUI, press **i** to access the command prompt.

2. From the TUI command prompt, type `start stream` and press **Enter**.

3. Verify that the stream is running.

4. From the Configure Channels app, from the **Sources** tab, click **+ Source**.

5. From the **Add Source** pane, click the **CloudLink** list and then click the name of your CloudLink.

6. In the **IP Address** field, enter the CloudLink multicast IP address. In the **Port** field, enter the CloudLink multicast port number. Click **Connect**.

7. After the connection is made, click **Choose From 1 Programs** and then click the only channel in the Choose From 1 Programs list.

8. Click the **Play Video** button.

9. Verify that the video plays, and then, from the video player, click **Close**.

10. From the Add Source pane, click **Cancel**.

11. From the CloudLink, press **i** to access the TUI command prompt.

12. From the command prompt, type stop stream and press **Enter**.
