## Platform Updates, Maintenance and Notifications

In order to fulfill our monthly uptime commitment, Harmonic will need to conduct periodic maintenance and upgrade of the service.

* Periodic maintenance and upgrade will be performed within the default maintenance windows as published on the Hub. If any other "scheduled maintenance window" is required, Harmonic will notify customers in advance with a minimum advance notice of 3 business days.

* Upgrades usually follow the blue/green paradigm.

  * During which we test the VOS360 from its input to the output demarcation point. But actual subscriber video player interop testing falls outside of the terms of this service.

  * Typically the blue and green runtimes are kept running for 24 hours after the upgrade, to help in case of issues. After 24 hours we decommission the VOS360 with the previous version.

* As for the normal VOS360 service, the preferred and fastest method of contact is through our Support conversation hosting in Slack and start via the Harmonic [Hub](http://hub.harmonicinc.com). Dedicated point of contact may be provided upon request.

Occasionally maintenance may require certain features to be disabled on a temporary basis. If this happens, we will use our Incident process to inform you what to expect.

## Sandbox and Beta environment

It is customary for our customers and technology partners that perform integration testing with our SaaS, to require a specific or dedicated "**sandbox**" environment. Harmonic is capable of supplying such a dedicated sub-system upon request.

Harmonic believes that obtaining customer feedback during development is critical to developing best-in-class video delivery solutions. As part of our agile development lifecycle, we periodically release new features to customers for their feedback and testing. Any features marked as **beta"** are intended as early access for use in non-production environments. Use of beta features means that you agree to provide testing and feedback of these features to help Harmonic improve our product. Beta features and add-ons do not guarantee their interfaces. This means that developers doing active development may find that versions and APIs change during the beta process, potentially breaking their application if they do not stay on top of the changes.

VOS360 SaaS usage of sandbox environment and features that are in beta are charged normally unless explicitly stated otherwise.
