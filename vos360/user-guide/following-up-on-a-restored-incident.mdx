Once any active incident is restored, the Slack communication channel will be closed and archived. It is available for future review, if desired.

## Slack channel archive

The Slack channel shall be archived the moment the incident is restored. From this moment on, our Support teams (DevOps T1, SRE, and R\&D) will be in charge of the root cause analysis investigation.

## Root cause analysis

Any support ticket (opened either from Harmonic Support or from your side) is thoroughly investigated until the root cause is determined.

Any information and updates will be visible in the Hub "*View cases/conversations*" section.

## Follow-up on restored cases and post-incident communication

If you want to follow up on the root cause investigation or need to leave some questions for our teams, you are able to do it via the Hub > Support section.

### How to check current investigation status and case updates

1. In the Hub, go to **Support** section > **View cases/conversations**

2. Check the list of all your current and past cases. You may filter cases per status (*Open, Closed, New, Responded, Restored, Resolved, Processing, All*).\
   ![](/images/vos360/85e07706f7ba8ff7f761cd87.png)

3. Click on any case number to check further information such as the **Root Cause** or the current **Plan of action** of the case.

### How to communicate with Harmonic Support once the Slack channel is closed

1. In the Hub, go to **Support** section > **View cases/conversations**

2. Check the list of all your current and past cases. You can leave a comment with questions for the assigned engineer **only on non-closed** cases (filter with *Restored, Resolved, Processing&#x20;*&#x73;tatus).

3. Click on any case number and scroll down to the **Comments** section and click on "Add comment". You can also add attachments if necessary.\
   ![](/images/vos360/2ce545c5bebacfcf4a13bd53.png)

4. This comment will be received by the engineer in charge of the case investigation and will be responded to via the Comments section.
