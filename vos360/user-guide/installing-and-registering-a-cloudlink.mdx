</Note>

Propagate video to VOS360 by installing a CloudLink, then register your newly installed CloudLink to a VOS360.

<Note>
  **Note:&#x20;**&#x48;armonic recommends that you use the Client Credentials flow to register CloudLink instances. Follow instructions for [Registering an OAuth client application](/vos360/user-guide/registering-an-oauth-client-application), and then complete Cloudlink registration.
</Note>
