# Configure the video processing parameters

## Video prefiltering

[Section titled “Video prefiltering”](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/media-processing/video-processing/#video-prefiltering)

To configure the video prefiltering parameters, follow these steps:

1. From the **Media Processing** tab, click the arrow at the right of the **Video Pre Filtering** section. The video prefilters are displayed.

2. You can [configure the video prefilters](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/parameters/media-processing/video-processing#video-prefiltering).

3. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.

## Video adjustment

[Section titled “Video adjustment”](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/media-processing/video-processing/#video-adjustment)

To configure the video adjustment parameters, follow these steps:

1. From the **Media Processing** tab, click the arrow at the right of the **Video Adjustment** section. The video prefilters are displayed.

2. You can configure the [video adjustment parameters](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/parameters/media-processing/video-processing#video-adjustment).

3. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.

## Image on frame freeze

[Section titled “Image on frame freeze”](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/media-processing/video-processing/#image-on-frame-freeze)

To configure a default image that will be displayed in case of frame freeze, follow these steps:

1. From the **Media processing** tab, go to the **Freeze frame management** section.

2. Tick the **Activate** checkbox.

3. [Set the appropriate value for each parameter](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/parameters/media-processing/video-processing#freeze-frame-management).

4. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.

## Video metadata

[Section titled “Video metadata”](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/media-processing/video-processing/#video-metadata)

To configure the video metadata parameters, follow these steps:

1. From the **Media Processing** tab, click the arrow at the right of the **Video Metadata** section.

2. You can select the **Timecode** source.

3. You can [insert this timecode in the SEI](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/parameters/encoding/video#stream-metadata-settings).

4. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.

## Video watermarking

[Section titled “Video watermarking”](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/media-processing/video-processing/#video-watermarking)

1. From the **Media Processing** > **Video processing** tab, go to the **Watermarking** section.

2. Tick the **Activate** checkbox then [set the appropriate value for each parameter](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/parameters/media-processing/video-processing#video-watermark-parameters).

3. Click **Ok** to validate.

4. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.
