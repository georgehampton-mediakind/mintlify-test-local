# Configure subtitle burn-in

You can burn DVB or Teletext subtitles into the video stream.

You must have defined one or more DVB or Teletext subtitle(s) (see [Configure input subtitle selection](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/input/subtitle)).

1. From the **Encoding** tab, go to the **Subtitle burn-in configuration** section.

2. Click **Add** to create a new subtitle burn-in configuration or click ![Edit][base64-image] to edit an existing one. The **Subtitle burn-in configuration** window is displayed.

3. In the **input** value, select the DVB or Teletext subtitle(s) that you previously created from the **Input** tab.

4. Configure the video encoding:

 1. For each video stream, click ![Edit][base64-image] then go to the **Subtitle settings** section and select a subtitle burn-in that you just created.

 ![el cfig encoding video subtitle burnin](https://docs.mediakind.com/_astro/el_cfig_encoding_video_subtitle_burnin.BbFeiP1e_b0JD3.webp)

 2. Click **OK** to validate.

5. Click **Save and continue** to save your modifications and go to another tab.

6. [Add subtitle(s) to output stream(s)](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/live-encoder/configure/output/configure-output#define-the-output-stream-composition).
