After a feed has been created, you must configure each service within the feed individually to build the complete MK.IO Beam contribution chain. The feed components work together to encode, multiplex, and deliver your live content to its destination.

<Warning>
  When editing component parameters, do not modify any interfaces configured to use the `tap0` or `lo` interfaces. These are used internally to pass data between services.
</Warning>

## Feed Components Overview

A standard contribution feed on MK.IO Beam consists of the following configurable components:

- [**Live Encoder**](/beam/contribution/live-encoder) — required. Compresses the baseband input into an MPEG Transport Stream.
- [**Multiplexer**](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/multiplexer) — optional. Combines multiple encoded services into a single transport stream.
- [**Satellite Modulator**](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/contribution/configure-feed-components/satellite-modulator) — optional. Modulates the transport stream for satellite uplink.

The Reliable Transport service is **not** included in any of the MK.IO Beam feed templates. It must be added separately via the **Services** page. See [Configure a Reliable Transport Service](#configure-a-reliable-transport-service) below.

---

## Configure the Multiplexer Service

The Multiplexer service combines multiple encoded services into a single MPEG Transport Stream output. For detailed information and all configuration parameters, refer to the [Multiplexer documentation](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/multiplexer).

## Configure a Reliable Transport Service

Although the Reliable Transport service is not included in all MK.IO Beam feed templates, you can add one separately and connect it to the input or output of a feed. A common use case is adding Reliable Transport to the output of an Encoder and Multiplexer feed to provide SRT, Zixi, or RIST output over an unmanaged IP network.

To create a Reliable Transport service, see [how to create a new Reliable Transport service](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/reliable-transport/service-management#create-a-new-reliable-transport-service).

### Configure Reliable Transport for Output

If you are configuring a Reliable Transport service to take the output from another service and deliver it over a reliable transport protocol, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the Output Mode">
    In the **Output** section of the Reliable Transport service configuration, set the **Output Mode** to **SRT Caller** (or the appropriate mode for your protocol).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match the Upstream Service Output">
    Configure the **Network Interface**, **Stream Address**, and **Port** with the same values as the upstream service's output. This connects the Reliable Transport service to receive from the encoder or multiplexer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save and exit** to finalize the configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For the full set of SRT, Zixi, and RIST configuration options — including listener and caller modes, encryption, and link characteristics — see [Reliable Transport Configuration](https://docs.mediakind.com/beam/reliable-transport/service-management).
</Tip>
