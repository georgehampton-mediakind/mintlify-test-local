MK.IO Beam for contribution is engineered to meet the demands of modern live content acquisition, delivering pristine video quality with maximum efficiency. Its flexible architecture allows for field reconfiguration for IP or satellite contribution, supporting up to 4:2:2 10-bit UHD across a range of server options. Beam is ideally suited to live events, where consumption-based pricing gives you the flexibility to scale costs to actual usage.

MK.IO Beam connects to the cloud, enabling direct and automated control, monitoring, and lifecycle management via the MK.IO cloud portal for efficient operations at scale.

## How Contribution Works

A contribution workflow on MK.IO Beam compresses a live video source — SDI, SMPTE ST 2110, or IP — into a compact, high-quality stream for delivery from a field location to a production facility or broadcast studio. The processing chain consists of feed components that you configure individually:

- **Live Encoder** — compresses the baseband input using HEVC, H.264, MPEG-2, or JPEG-XS encoding at up to 4:2:2 10-bit UHD. Handles video, audio, and data including SCTE-35 pass-through.
- **Multiplexer** (optional) — combines multiple encoded services into a single MPEG Transport Stream for efficient delivery.
- **Reliable Transport** (optional, added separately) — wraps the transport stream in SRT, Zixi, or RIST for resilient delivery over unmanaged IP networks.
- **Satellite Modulator** (optional) — modulates the transport stream to L-band RF for satellite uplink (DVB-S, DVB-S2, DVB-S2X).
