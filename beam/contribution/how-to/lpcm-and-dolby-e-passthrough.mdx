---
title: Set up LPCM and Dolby-E pass-through
sidebarTitle: LCM and Dolby E Passthrough
---

<span id="_top" className="mk-legacy-anchor" />

LPCM and Dolby-E inputs on SDI can be passed through and carried on the TS as SMPTE-302 and Dolby-E respectively by selecting the **SMPTE-302 / Dolby E Pass-Through** codec from Live Encoder **Encoding** > **Audio encoding** section (see [Configure the audio encoding parameters](/beam/live-encoder/configure/encoding/audio-encoding)).

![ce1 el lpcm dolby passthrough](/ce1-img/ce1_el_lpcm_dolby_passthrough.png)

This is supported for both SDI and SMPTE ST 2110 ingests.
