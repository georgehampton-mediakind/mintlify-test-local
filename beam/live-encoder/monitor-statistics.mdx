You can retrieve and monitor detailed statistics information on a service.

<Info>
  The <img src="/icons/ui/chart.svg" alt="Chart" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> icon is only accessible when a Live encoding servive is associated to a server and then running.
</Info>

1. From the **Services** page, identify the service that you want to monitor and click the related <img src="/icons/ui/chart.svg" alt="Chart" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> icon. The **Statistics** page displays.

2. You can navigate between three tabs:

* [**Media info**](/beam/live-encoder/monitor-statistics#media-info)
* [**Input monitoring**](/beam/live-encoder/monitor-statistics#input-monitoring)
* [**Outputs**](/beam/live-encoder/monitor-statistics#outputs).

<Info>
  You can customize the information displayed such as activating or deactivating the auto refresh by clicking the **Auto refresh** button.
</Info>

## Media info

From the **Media info** tab, you can view the following information for each program:

* Video statistics
* Audio statistics
* Preview of the video
* List of other available programs

![MFVP EL service statistics mediainfo](/_machine-assets/ca8d164a2c596a68e85639e6a76062436b1e188f057c9fa536c8561e948dc9b0.png)
*Example of Service Statistics>Media info*

## Input monitoring

From the **Input monitoring info** tab, you can view the following information:

* Quality indicators (ETR290)
* RTP quality indicators
* FEC quality indicators
* Input CC Errors
* Splice schedule

![MFVP EL service statistics input](/_machine-assets/19bbdf2199dce329229b4e4fa68081cf79129c25dbf30a44cdda657cb3180f96.png)
*Example of Service Statistics>Input monitoring*

## Outputs

From the **Outputs** tab, you can view the following information for each output:

* PID number
* Media type (audio, video, subtitles, metadata)
* CC errors
* Information

![MFVP EL service statistics output](/_machine-assets/f87062f31522ae6131db7f25a919bcd0feb272f045ee47a725bf58f3fe9459dd.png)
*Example of Service Statistics>Output monitoring*

* Once you have configured an automated blackout and started a service, you can manually [activate or deactivate the blackout](/beam/live-encoder/configure/encoding/blackout-encoding#manually-activate-or-deactivate-a-blackout).
* Once you have activated and configured the [Switch to CBR option](/beam/live-encoder/parameters/encoding/video#switch-to-cbr-parameters), and started the service, you can manually switch from Statmux to CBR (**Switch to CBR** button) or switch back from CBR to Statmux (**Switch to Statmux** button).

  ![EL service statistics output switch to CBR](/_machine-assets/e9585a8571bb82122831c13660c6590b80461df588986156b0850075d09d4f40.png)
  *Example of Service Statistics>Outputs with Switch to CBR option activated*
