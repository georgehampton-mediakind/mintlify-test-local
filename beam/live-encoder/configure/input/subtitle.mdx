To configure the input stream subtitle parameters, follow these steps:

1. Display Services.

2. Click <img src="/icons/ui/edit.svg" alt="Edit" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> to edit the service.

3. From the **Input** tab, scroll to **Input stream selection** > **Subtitle ID** and click **Add**.

4. [Set Subtitles options](/beam/live-encoder/parameters/input/subtitle), then click **OK** to save.

Subtitles are automatically added to the **Encoding** tab in pass-through only.

* You can [configure subtitle encoding parameters](/beam/live-encoder/configure/encoding/subtitles-encoding).
* You can [configure subtitle burn-in](/beam/live-encoder/configure/encoding/subtitle-burnin-encoding).
* You can [add subtitles to output streams](/beam/live-encoder/configure/output/configure-output#define-the-output-stream-composition).
