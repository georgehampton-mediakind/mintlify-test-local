You can burn DVB or Teletext subtitles into the video stream.

You must have defined one or more DVB or Teletext subtitle(s) (see [Configure input subtitle selection](/beam/live-encoder/configure/input/subtitle)).

1. From the **Encoding** tab, go to the **Subtitle burn-in configuration** section.

2. Click **Add** to create a new subtitle burn-in configuration or click <img src="/icons/ui/edit.svg" alt="Edit" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> to edit an existing one. The **Subtitle burn-in configuration** window is displayed.

3. In the **input** value, select the DVB or Teletext subtitle(s) that you previously created from the **Input** tab.

4. Configure the video encoding:

   1. For each video stream, click <img src="/icons/ui/edit.svg" alt="Edit" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> then go to the **Subtitle settings** section and select a subtitle burn-in that you just created.

      ![el cfig encoding video subtitle burnin](/_machine-assets/ed51f0494f866081c004bc5db27f608e7f1bc9bc321cf4fe3e82302b645edce8.png)

   2. Click **OK** to validate.

5. Click **Save and continue** to save your modifications and go to another tab.

6. [Add subtitle(s) to output stream(s)](/beam/live-encoder/configure/output/configure-output#define-the-output-stream-composition).
