1. From the **Encoding** tab, go to the **Logo insertion** section.

2. Tick the **Activate** checkbox. New [parameters](/beam/live-encoder/parameters/encoding/logo-insertion) are displayed.

3. Enter and/or select the appropriate value for each parameter.

4. Click **Save and continue** or **Save and exit** to save your changes.
