## Creating a new Live Packaging service

To create a new Live Packaging service, follow these steps:

1. Access the MK.IO Beam user interface and click **Services** in the left panel.

2. Click the **Add service** button, then select **Live Packaging**.

3. You can configure the [parameters](/beam/packager/parameters/live).

## Starting a Live Packaging service

**To start a Live Packaging service, you need to have previously configured**:

* A Catalog that contains at least one output ([Adding an output to a Catalog](/beam/packager/catalog#adding-an-output-to-a-catalog)).
* A Live Packaging service that:
  * contains at least one source and a publishing point ([DVR](/beam/packager/parameters/live#dvr)).
  * is linked to an existing Catalog.

**To start a Live packaging service, follow these steps:**

1. Access the MK.IO Beam user interface then click **Services** in the left panel.

2. Assign the service to a server. (Remember: A server must be assigned to Start the service.)

3. Click the <img src="/icons/ui/play-outline.svg" alt="Play outline" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> button in the **Actions** column to start the related service.

The service status turns from **stopped** to **started**. The service starts publishing the DVR as configured.

## Stopping a Live Packaging service

<Warning>
  In a solution deployment with  servers, any Live Packaging service associated to a Live Encoding service must be stopped and started again after a modification of the Live Encoding configuration. The associated DVR must also be deleted.
</Warning>

To stop a Live Packaging service, follow these steps:

1. Go to the **Actions** column.

2. Click the <img src="/icons/ui/stop-outline.svg" alt="Stop outline" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> button to stop the related service.

The service status turns from **started** to **stopped**. The service stops publishing the DVR.

The origin server will return 404 errors 60 seconds after the DVR is stopped.

## Deleting a Live Packaging service and managing DVR

Depending on the context, deleting a Live Packaging service by clicking <img src="/icons/ui/delete.svg" alt="Delete" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> in the **Actions** column has different behaviors and results.

<Warning>
  A Live Packaging service with the <img src="/icons/ui/play-outline.svg" alt="Play outline" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> (started) status cannot be deleted. Click the <img src="/icons/ui/stop-outline.svg" alt="Stop outline" className="mk-ui-icon" width="16" height="16" /> button to stop the service.
</Warning>

### The Live Packaging service has no server assigned

* The service configuration is deleted and removed from the service list. It cannot be recovered after deletion.
* The DVR generated by the service remains on the storage point.
