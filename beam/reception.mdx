<Note>
  **Check which interface you are using**

  The guides linked from this page configure MK.IO Beam through the **Advanced** interface, where a workflow is a **feed** made up of individual services. If your device runs the **Essentials** interface, build the workflow as a **channel** instead: see [Create a channel](/beam/essentials/create-a-channel). Both interfaces control the same settings underneath, so these pages stay useful as reference for the detail behind a channel.
</Note>

**MK.IO Beam for reception** is engineered to meet the evolving demands of processing at the edge of your network, delivering high-quality video and audio decode with maximum efficiency. Its flexible architecture allows for seamless integration into existing workflows, providing a future-proof solution for content turnaround and distribution.
